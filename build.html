Aircraft Design and Performance — Team of 4Spring 2026
Conceptual Design

Ship-Launched Multi-Domain ISR UAV

ENGR 3802 · Advisor: Dr. Gaurav Sharma · Responsible for airfoil selection, CAD/CFD, and final sizing
  • Designed a ship-launched, tailless flying-wing UAV for intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) in both the air and underwater: 15.4 ft span, 40° leading-edge sweep, 2,050 lb takeoff weight, and a single internal PW610 turbofan
  • Met every requirement with margin: 1,060 NMi range against 1,000 NMi, 47 min loiter against 30 min, and 89 kt stall speed against a 97 kt limit, with a cruise L/D of 13.5 at Mach 0.7
  • Developed the dual-domain transition: sealing doors close the engine inlet and exhaust, the aircraft lands on water in a deep stall at about 10° pitch, wing-root fuel tanks flood to serve as ballast, and retractable wingtip thrusters drive underwater transit to a recovery vessel
  • Selected a spanwise airfoil blend from a thick centerbody to a thin tip for transonic efficiency and internal volume, and ran four-concept trade studies on aerodynamic efficiency, stealth, structural complexity, stability, and dual-domain capability
  • Chose a hybrid CFRP and aluminum 5083 structure with Inconel in the exhaust region and EPDM seals, and identified water-landing slamming loads as the primary risk for follow-on FEA and drop testing

GIS Mapping: Dispersed Camp Map and 3D Hiking Terrain Map

Web maps built from open geospatial data, terrain elevation data, and GPS metadata
