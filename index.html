Project and Design Portfolio

I'm a junior in Mechanical Engineering, minoring in Aerospace, at Kennesaw State University, graduating May 2027. My work splits between hands-on hardware including rocket propulsion, autonomous aircraft, and ground support equipment. As well as CFD research validated against wind-tunnel and experimental data. I served as Vice President of the Kennesaw Rocketry Team through June 2026. I spent this past summer as a Mechanical Design Engineer Intern at Textron GSE, and am currently a UAV Flight Control Intern at NASA Glenn Research Center.
