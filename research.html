Tilt-Wing Propeller Modeling with Experimental Validation

Mid-fidelity FlightStream vs. blade element theory vs. WindShape wind-tunnel data
  • Modeling an APC 16×10E propeller for tilt-wing and tilt-prop VTOL UAVs in Simcenter FlightStream by physically rotating the blade model with the unsteady surface-vorticity solver, compared against a blade element model with Pitt–Peters dynamic inflow
  • Validating against six-component force-balance measurements from the WindShape facility at OSU–Tulsa, covering thrust angle inclinations from 0° to 180° in 15° steps at 3000, 4000, and 5000 RPM
  • Verified the reconstructed blade geometry against a 3D scan of the test propeller, and is running sensitivity studies on time step, mesh, and wake settings before the full test matrix
